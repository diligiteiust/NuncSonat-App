# Control Music on another Mac

Premium can control Music on another Mac that is running Nunc Sonat. Both Macs need to be on the same network, and both need Local Network permission.

## On the Mac that will be controlled

1. Open Settings → General.
2. Turn on **Allow other Macs to control this Mac**.
3. A 6-digit pairing code appears. Leave that Mac awake with Nunc Sonat running.

This also needs Automation access for Music on that Mac.

## On the Mac you are sitting at

1. Open the menu. The other Mac appears under **Remotes**.
2. Choose it and tap **Pair**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code shown on the other Mac.

After that, play, pause, skip, volume, and the playlist on this Mac control Music over there. The pairing is remembered. **Revoke** on either Mac removes it. Pairing again shows a new code.

Without Premium the other Mac still appears in the list, with a Premium badge. Choosing it opens the upgrade panel.
