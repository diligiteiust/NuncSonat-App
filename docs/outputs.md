# AirPlay outputs

Speakers that an Apple TV or HomePod is playing to show under **AirPlay speakers & others**. Sonos, another Mac, and an iPad appear here. They are outputs. They are not paired, and they have no Pair button.

## Main volume

The slider in the menu and the mini player is the group volume, the same control the iPhone remote uses for that AirPlay group. It is free.

## Choosing outputs

Checking or unchecking an output chooses where the audio goes. That is a Premium feature. Without a license the rows stay visible, with a Premium badge. Choosing one opens the upgrade panel.

## Volume on each speaker

When the Apple TV names the receivers, each included output has its own volume slider. Per-speaker volume is Premium. The group slider above it stays available.

## What you cannot pair

An AirPlay speaker is not a remote. To change what is playing, pair the Apple TV or HomePod that is sending the audio, then use these rows only to choose speakers and, with Premium, their levels.
